# Pricing Cards

> Week 04 · `card` · #23

Pricing cards with a plan finder: set team size and storage, and the cheapest plan that fits is marked "Best fit for you" while too-small plans say why. Plus a monthly/yearly toggle with odometer prices.

- Live demo: https://lofistack-sayan.netlify.app/components/pricing-cards
- Source: https://github.com/sayan30082000/LofiStack-90-day-challenge/blob/main/components/ui/pricing-cards.tsx
- Build prompt: https://lofistack-sayan.netlify.app/components/pricing-cards#prompt
